How the comparison and recommendations work

The matrix shows evidence roles. Recommendations use the selected claim, held capabilities and applicable evidence joins. A primary observation can require other records before a claim qualifies.

A measured minimum confirms all eligible positives with zero recorded false confirmations. A marginal gain compares counts for the same claim, cohort and cutoff. An unknown comparison stays unscored. Recorded sufficiency at a cutoff does not establish alert latency.

Shapley averages the gains across all recorded subsets when the table supports it. Where comparable data is missing, use the cited qualitative dependencies. Effort comparisons use your nonoverlapping estimates. Priority weights choose the leading tier.
