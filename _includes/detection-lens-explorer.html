Source-reported covers behavior described in outside research. It helps explain possible attack paths and stays out of the lab counts.

Measured comes from a controlled lab. Synthetic comes from replaying modeled evidence. Depending on the study, supplemental telemetry can add actor or object identity, policy, transaction records, or an independent record of the outcome.

A confirmed effect tells you what happened. A confirmed causal link takes separate evidence tying that effect to an earlier action.
